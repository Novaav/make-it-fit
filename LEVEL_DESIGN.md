# Verified level specifications

Source of truth for the data: `VERIFIED_LEVELS.json`, generated by `verify_levels.py` (exhaustive search over all legal rotations and positions). Independently re-derived by `src/engine/enumerate.test.ts`, which uses the TypeScript engine's own geometry and fails if the numbers below drift. Human difficulty and fun are NOT verified; they need playtesting.

Conventions: weights are integer **grams** in the data and shown as kilograms; coordinates are zero-based, x right, y down; one grid cell is roughly 9 cm. The allowance applies to the weight of the illustrated items (the game does not model the empty suitcase); a total **equal** to the allowance succeeds. From Level 2 each selectable item carries a **Chaos** value; a level is won by reaching the target Chaos within the allowance (and packing any mandatory items). Mandatory items carry no Chaos.

## Level 1 — Pack Like a Grown-Up
Board 5×6 (30 cells), no allowance, 5 required items (21 cells, 3.55 kg), no optional items. Each item also carries a Chaos value for display only (T-shirt 2, jeans 2, sneakers 3, toiletries 1, charger 1; total 9): the Chaos total counts up as items are packed, but there is no target and the level is still won by packing all five.

| Item | Cells | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| T-shirt (`shirt`) | 4 (T) | 250 g |
| Jeans (`jeans`) | 6 (line) | 700 g |
| Sneakers (`shoes`) | 5 (U) | 1.2 kg |
| Toiletries (`toiletries`) | 4 (2×2) | 1.0 kg |
| Laptop charger (`charger`) | 2 | 400 g |

Proven: solvable; **rotation is mandatory** (the 6-long jeans only fit standing up on a 5-wide board; the no-rotation variant is unsatisfiable); 45,408 distinct packings of the required items.

Both the space and the weight limit bind.

## Level 3 — Excess Baggage
Board 7×6 (42 cells), allowance **20.5 kg**, two mandatory items (Hotel Painting 3×3, 4 kg; Laundry bag 3×2, 5 kg), 10 selectable items. Win at **24 Chaos**; perfect **29**.

Selectable items (cells, weight, Chaos): party outfit (4, 900 g, 2), hangover kit (4, 800 g, 3), overpriced heels (5, 1.3 kg, 3), hotel lamp (4, 3.6 kg, 3), marble foot (5, 3.6 kg, 7), garden gnome (6, 6.5 kg, 9), wheel of cheese (4, 2.8 kg, 5), Badcardi rum (3, 1.5 kg, 5), bathrobe (5, 1.3 kg, 4), socks (2, 150 g, 3).

Verified (`verify_levels.py` produces these figures, CI fails if the committed data differs, and `src/engine/enumerate.test.ts` re-derives the item sets in TypeScript with the engine's own shape and rotation code, so it checks the search and the data rather than the rotation maths): **532** legal item combinations (within the allowance and packable), **37** of which win, **1** perfect combination (outfit, hangover kit, marble foot, cheese, rum, bathrobe, socks: 29 Chaos, 20.05 kg, fills all 42 cells). Two further figures came from a one-off audit script that is not in the repository and are not re-checked by the tests: 5,516 physical arrangements of that perfect set, none without rotation, and the greedy and hill-climb difficulty proxies. Weight and space both bind; the gnome is a real but imperfect option (it appears in 6 winning combinations and in no perfect one).

Design notes: the painting is a believable 3×3 rectangle (a larger or oddly notched shape was tested and rejected as contrived). With it the perfect pack must rotate, "most chaotic first" no longer wins, and no mandatory layout dead-ends a normal win (65% of mandatory layouts do block the perfect pack, which is the planning puzzle).

## Scoring rules
Win = every mandatory item packed, weight at most the allowance (exactly equal is fine) and Chaos at least the target (80% of perfect, rounded up). There is no partial credit and no star bonus. Chaos values are joke-driven design numbers, not measurements.

## Progression (design judgment, unplaytested)
L1 teaches fitting and rotation; L2 adds a visible weight ceiling and Chaos with many routes; L3 adds mandatory baggage, a tighter ceiling relative to value and a trap item. Chaos scoring was chosen over an optional-count bonus after the exhaustive audit; difficulty proxies (greedy and hill-climb strategies) are not human difficulty and need playtesting.

## History
- The earlier mandatory-plus-optional-star design (L2 7 kg, L3 23 kg) was replaced by the Chaos value-target design.
- Earlier Level 2 and Level 3 item sets are kept in Git history.
