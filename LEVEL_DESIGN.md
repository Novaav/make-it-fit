# Verified level specifications

Source of truth for the data: `VERIFIED_LEVELS.json`, generated by `verify_levels.py` (exhaustive search over all legal rotations and positions). Independently re-derived by `src/engine/enumerate.test.ts`, which uses the TypeScript engine's own geometry and fails if the numbers below drift. Human difficulty and fun are NOT verified; they need playtesting.

Conventions: weights are integer **grams** in the data and shown as kilograms; coordinates are zero-based, x right, y down; one grid cell is roughly 9 cm. The allowance applies to the weight of the illustrated items (the game does not model the empty suitcase); a total **equal** to the allowance succeeds. Optional ("★") items never affect completion; the verified best bonus is stored in `src/levelMeta.ts` (`BEST_OPTIONAL`).

## Level 1 — Pack Like a Grown-Up
Board 5×6 (30 cells), no allowance, 5 required items (21 cells, 3.55 kg), no optional items.

| Item | Cells | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| T-shirt (`shirt`) | 4 (T) | 250 g |
| Jeans (`jeans`) | 6 (line) | 700 g |
| Sneakers (`shoes`) | 5 (U) | 1.2 kg |
| Toiletries (`toiletries`) | 4 (2×2) | 1.0 kg |
| Laptop charger (`charger`) | 2 | 400 g |

Proven: solvable; **rotation is mandatory** (the 6-long jeans only fit standing up on a 5-wide board; the no-rotation variant is unsatisfiable); 45,408 distinct packings of the required items.

## Level 2 — Carry-On, Questionable Decisions
Board 6×5 (30 cells), allowance **7 kg**, 4 required items (16 cells, 3.60 kg), 6 optional.

| Item | Role | Cells | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Party outfit (`outfit`) | required | 4 (T) | 900 g |
| Toiletries (`toiletries`) | required | 4 (2×2) | 1.0 kg |
| Red-soled heels (`heels`) | required | 5 (U) | 1.3 kg |
| Pocket massager (`vibrator`) | required | 3 (line) | 400 g |
| Party speaker (`speaker`) | optional | 2 (line) | 1.5 kg |
| Disco ball (`disco_ball`) | optional | 4 (2×2) | 1.5 kg |
| Framed photo of the ex (`frame`) | optional | 6 (2×3) | 1.2 kg |
| Inflatable flamingo (`flamingo`) | optional | 6 (3×2) | 850 g |
| Feather boa (`boa`) | optional | 4 (line) | 300 g |
| Ring light and tripod (`ringlight`) | optional | 5 (T) | 800 g |

Proven: maximum bonus **3**; exactly **8** legal three-item sets (speaker+disco ball+boa, speaker+frame+boa, speaker+flamingo+boa, speaker+flamingo+ring light, speaker+boa+ring light, disco ball+frame+boa, disco ball+flamingo+boa, disco ball+boa+ring light); all 15 pairs are legal; 8 of 20 triples are legal; no four-item set is legal. Both constraints bind: 5 optional sets fit on the board but are over 7 kg, and 6 are light enough but cannot be packed. Required items alone: 108,156 packings.

## Level 3 — Excess Baggage
Board 8×6 (48 cells), allowance **23 kg**, 5 required items (29 cells, 12.00 kg), 7 optional.

| Item | Role | Cells | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cardboard cutout (`cutout`) | required | 10 | 4.0 kg |
| Laundry bag (`laundry`) | required | 6 (3×2) | 5.0 kg |
| Party outfit (`outfit`) | required | 4 (T) | 900 g |
| Hangover kit (`hangover`) | required | 4 (2×2) | 800 g |
| Red-soled heels (`heels`) | required | 5 (U) | 1.3 kg |
| Hotel lamp (`hotel_lamp`) | optional | 4 (L) | 3.6 kg |
| Ceramic foot (`ceramic_foot`) | optional | 5 (U) | 3.2 kg |
| Concrete garden gnome (`gnome`) | optional | 6 (2×3) | 6.5 kg |
| Wheel of cheese (`cheese`) | optional | 4 (2×2) | 2.8 kg |
| Bottle of local spirit (`liquor`) | optional | 3 (line) | 1.5 kg |
| Hotel bathrobe (`robe`) | optional | 5 (folded) | 1.3 kg |
| Emergency socks (`emergency_socks`) | optional | 2 (line) | 150 g |

Proven: maximum bonus **5** (the "Perfect pack", optional; Level 2's top tier is labelled "Maximum bonus") with exactly **three** legal sets, each filling 47-48 cells: hotel lamp+ceramic foot+liquor+robe+socks (21.75 kg), hotel lamp+cheese+liquor+robe+socks (21.35 kg), ceramic foot+cheese+liquor+robe+socks (20.95 kg). 17 legal four-item sets and 29 of 35 triples are legal. Both constraints bind: 23 optional sets fit on the board but are over 23 kg. A real packing of the required items plus the gnome, ceramic foot and robe weighs exactly 23.000 kg (a unit test pins that exact-limit success).

## Progression (design judgment, unplaytested)
L1 teaches fitting and rotation with no weights; L2 adds a visible ceiling and a bonus with many routes; L3 makes weight and space interlock on a 0-5 bonus ladder. Completing a level needs only the required items within the allowance.

- Level 2 was previously 6×5 at 15 units and Level 3 6×5 at 24 units with the passport, DVD, phone charger and other items (kept in Git history); replaced by this redesign after the gameplay audit.
